Design Theory

DOCUMENTATION · METHODOLOGY · COMMUNICATION

Mastery of the complete game design documentation pipeline — from the high-concept One Pager to the comprehensive Game Design Document. The One Pager captures the essence of the concept with clarity and focus; the Ten Pager structures the creative vision and core mechanics in detail; full GDDs cover systems, economy, progression, and tone; and Level Design Documents map every spatial and interaction blueprint. Flow diagrams are used to chart system logic, player decision trees, and state transitions. Every deliverable is built to communicate vision effectively to teams, stakeholders, and production pipelines.

COMPETENCIES
